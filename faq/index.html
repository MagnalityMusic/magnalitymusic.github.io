FAQ

Can I use your music in my game?

Yes. A standard licence is included when you purchase a music pack. This allows you to use the music in commercial and non-commercial projects, including games, videos, and streams.

Can I use the music commercially?

Yes. Commercial use is included with the standard licence.

Can I modify the music?

Yes. You can edit and modify the music to suit your project.

Can I use the same music in multiple projects?

Yes. Your standard licence allows unlimited projects.

Can I use the music in a soundtrack?

A separate soundtrack licence is required if you want to distribute the music as an official soundtrack or soundtrack DLC.

Can I include the music in a supporter or deluxe edition?

An Extended Licence allows the music to be included as part of a larger supporter, bonus, deluxe, or collector's package.

Do I need to credit Magnality?

Credit is appreciated but is not required unless otherwise stated by the specific licence.

Where can I purchase your music?

Magnality music is available through the stores linked on each product page.

Can I get a custom licence?

Yes. If your project has licensing requirements that aren't covered by the standard, extended, or soundtrack licences, get in touch through the Contact page.

Is your music AI generated?

No. All my music is hand-composed and produced by me!

Can content creators record gameplay with your music without copyright strikes?

Yes. None of my music is registered with ContentID so youtubers, streamers and other content creators can freely play your games without getting demonetised or copyright striked.


In the unlikely event that there are copyright issues please get in contact with me at magnalitymusic@gmail.com as this will be a false claim.
